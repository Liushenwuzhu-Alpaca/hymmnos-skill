# Hymmnos Example Sentences

Annotated examples from real Hymmnos songs and teaching materials. Each example includes the Hymmnos text, word-by-word gloss, English translation, and source.

## Canonical Teaching Sentence

**Was yea ra chs hymmnos mea**

| Word | Meaning | Function |
|------|---------|----------|
| Was | very intensely | Emotion Sound I (degree) |
| yea | happy | Emotion Sound II (type) |
| ra | want to continue | Emotion Sound III (desire) |
| chs | become | verb |
| hymmnos | song | object |
| mea | me/myself | object |

Translation: "I am delighted to express myself through song"
Dialect: Central Standard Note
Source: Hymmnoserver, EXA_PICO Wiki

Pastalie equivalent: `cEzE hymmnos/.` (same meaning, using c.z. = become + E = joy)

## Standard Hymmnos Examples

### First-Person Sentences

**Was yea ra sonwe mea hymmnos mea**
= I will be glad to sing my song to myself
Source: EXA_PICO Wiki

**Was yea erra melenas tes ar ciel**
= I will be eternally glad to give my love to this world
(tes = to; melenas = love; ar ciel = the world)
Source: Teaching material

**Was yea ra chs hymmnos yor**
= I will be glad to turn you into a song
Source: Teaching material

**Was yea ra na chs hymmnos yor**
= I will be glad for NOT turning you into a song (na negates chs)
Source: Teaching material

### Non-First-Person Sentences

**Wee ki ra hyma rre sasye pagle wart**
= I will concentrate on listening to the girl speaking words
(Wee=fairly, ki=focused, ra=continue, hyma=listen, rre=subject marker, sasye=girl, pagle=speak, wart=words)
Source: EXA_PICO Wiki

**Was yea ra rre hyma hymme**
= The bird sings happily (and I share that feeling)
Source: Teaching material

**Was ki ra rre lasye linen sasye**
= The boy sincerely speaks to the girl
Source: Teaching material

### Emotionless Sentences

**Faura yerwe murfan anw sol ciel**
= The little bird chirps her feelings to the world
(Faura=bird, yerwe=chirp, murfan=feelings, anw=to, sol=shining, ciel=world)
Source: EXEC_HARMONIUS

**Faura sonwe murfan anw sol ciel ee**
= The little bird sings her feelings to the people
(ee = people/great)
Source: EXEC_HARMONIUS

### Passive Voice

**deggeez anw ciel** = I betray the world (active)
**re deggeez art ciel** = I am betrayed by the world (passive; re + verb, art = by)
Source: EXEC_SPHILIA

**Was ki ra gyuss lir** = I embrace the light (active)
**Was ki ra re gyuss lir** = I am embraced by the light (passive)
Source: Teaching material

**Harr re heighte der gauzewiga oz ciel**
= She was stricken by the world's despair
(Harr=she, re=passive, heighte=injure, der=by, gauzewiga=despair, oz=of, ciel=world)
Source: EXEC_METAFALICA

### Negation (position controls scope)

**Was yea ra na chs hymmnos yor** = I will NOT turn you into a song
**Was yea ra chs na hymmnos yor** = Not a song, but something else
**Was yea ra chs hymmnos na yor** = Not you, but someone else
Source: Teaching material

**na yehah** = unhappiness (negating noun)
**na fowrlle heighte** = unfading scars
**sarla na layy** = song does not lie
Source: EXEC_SPHILIA

### Possession

**sarla mea** = my song (noun + pronoun)
**hyzik yor** = your body
**hymmnos oz faura** = song of the bird (oz = of)
**Aurica forlinden** = Aurica's village (proper noun + noun)
**chyet oz omnis** = chosen of all
Source: Teaching material

### Persistent Emotion Sounds

**Was yea erra 0x vvi.**
**chs hymmnos mea**
**hymme rre walasye hyma mea**
**sonwe anw sol ciel**
**rre sol ciel hyma hynne mea**
**1x AAs ixi.**
**Wee apea ra melenas yor**

Expanded (= all inner sentences carry "Was yea erra"):
1. I will be eternally happy to turn into a song
2. I will be eternally happy that the people hear me
3. I will be eternally happy to sing to the world
4. I will be eternally happy that the world hears me
5. (Outside keeper) Immersed in happiness to love you
Source: EXA_PICO Wiki

### Question Sentences

**Nn num ra whait irs ar ciel** = What exists in the sky?
**Nn num gagis whai hymma chs fwal** = Why did the bird become wings?
Source: Teaching material

### Special Storytelling Form

**Was yea ra vit bautifal faura anw dornpica en 1 dyyal nuih bexm**
= The beautiful little bird saw the seeds, and the first night came
(VSO form with emotion sound)
Source: EXEC_HIBERNATION

**Nn num gaya na ieeya crushue anw dornpica, kaguya**
= Kaguya doesn't wish to craft the seeds anymore
(Subject after comma for emphasis)
Source: EXEC_HIBERNATION

## Neopact Pastalie Examples

### Single-Word Sentences

**hEmmErYE/.** = I will happily sing for your happiness
(h.m.m.r. = sing; E = joy to self; YE = joy to you)

**hYAmmrA** = I will sing my best for you
(YA = to you; A = power/focus)

**hYImOmOrO** = I will sing of how I want you to suffer
(YI = suffering to you; O = rage)

**hEmEmArA** = I will sing as best I can to express my elation

**hLYImLYUmOrO** = I will sing angrily of bringing ruin and chaos upon the world
(LYI = ruin to all; LYU = unease to all; O = rage)

### With Explicit Objects

**fEwErYEn/.** = I lovingly embrace you (implied object)
**fEwErYEn Luca/.** = I lovingly embrace Luca (explicit object)

**hYAmmrA chroche/.** = I will sing my best for Cloche

**cEzE hymmnos/.** = I am delighted to express myself through song
**cYEzYE hymmnos/.** = You are delighted I am expressing you through song (YE targets you)

### Subject Definer (x.)

**xE rre cloche cEzE hymmnos/.**
= Cloche is delighted to become a song (and this makes me happy)
(x. with E = I feel happy about the subject)

**xI harr cEzE hymmnos/.**
= She is delighted to become a song (and I'm jealous)
(x. with I = I feel jealous)

**xE rre yorra cEzE hymmnos/.**
= YOU ALL are delighted to become a song (emphasized with rre + pronoun)

### Passive, Negation, Desire

**fEwErYEneh** = I am lovingly embraced by you (passive: -eh suffix)
**zz hEmmErYE/.** = I would NOT be delighted to sing (negation: zz prefix)
**zz arhou** = despair (negating "hope")
**hEmmrEaye** = want to sing (desire: -aye suffix)

### Nominalization and Functions

**aIuUkA zess pop v.a en d.z./.**
= Birth and death are like a bubble
(All bank periods kept = gerund/noun form; zess = like; pop = bubble)

**ishikawa -> jYOzAt METHOD_HYMME_ISHIKAWA_JANNE/.!**
**ishikawa! ishikawa! ishikawa! ishikawa!**
= Define "ishikawa" as the full METHOD command, then invoke it 4 times

### Hypothetical and Back-Reference

**Xc=hLYEmYEmArA -> cEzLYE hymmnos/.**
= If I sing, then I will become a song
(Xc= = conditional; -> = then)

**zz arhou, balduo, ujes, Oqejyu, xA rre <-x aYAuAkN kajya LYAglansee qejyu/.**
= Despair, darkness, ill will, hated people — they are necessary as well
(<-x = back-reference to the preceding list)

### Possessive Noun Prefixes

**Agasar** = my stuffed doll (A = my, with positive emotion)
**YAgasar** = your stuffed doll (YA = your)
**LYAgasar** = everyone's doll (LYA = belonging to all)
**Agasar_cloche** = Cloche's doll (A + noun + _name)

**wYEsA Agasar_luca/.**
= I will give you Luca's precious stuffed doll to make you happy
(w.s. = give; A prefix on gasar = Luca's, with positive feeling)

## Binasphere Chorus Example (EXEC_NULLASCENSION/.)

Encoded text:
```
=> RRHA RRHA GUWO Ax GAx PEx GIS A GAx TIE INNx
GIS NA GRAN GAx PAUL NOx TYUNY INI SAASH AR YANJE
CIEL EN INI LA ZAx AR HHA CIEL RRHA RRHA Ax GUWO
GA PEx GAx A TYUNY RA HARx AR CIEL
TES EN YORA INI CHYET WAx SOR GAx LAx TYUNx SYE LA
FORx GANx ART SA DAL FAYx WASSA RA CIEL
EXEC hymme 2x1/0>>01101010
```

Decoded Line 0: **Rrha apea gagis gran paul nosaash yanje en ini ar ciel**
= In this delightful trance, I feel the way will be opened for the goddess to purify this world forever

Decoded Line 1: **Rrha guwo gagis tie innna gatyuny ini ar ciel la zahha**
= In this trance of hatred, I shall tie this curse to the inside of my mind, then purify and advance this world

Second pair:
Line 0: **Rrha apea ra hartes yora chyet walasye forgandal wassa ciel**
= I will love your special ones because of the festival of the world

Line 1: **Rrha guwo ga gatyuny ar ciel en ini sor gatyunla art sa fayra**
= I shall purify this accursed world through the flames of hell

## EXEC_SPHILIA/. Key Lines

**kiafa hynne mea? pagle tes yor.** = Can you hear my voice? My voice calling to you.
**Fou paks ga kiafa hynne yor** = Yes, I can hear your voice (wary)
**Was yea ra pauwel en wael yor.** = I would give you further power and pleasure
**Yorr faf, so** = I can tell you're scared.
**Yorr nille mea** = You're like me.
**na fowrlle heighte** = Bearing unfading scars
**En yorr re deggeez art ciel** = Betrayed by the world
**Presia messe noce yor tes mea** = Tell me about yourself just as you are
