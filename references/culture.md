## Writing System

- Standardized A.D. 3018 by El Elemia Tower Administration Bureau
- 71 characters: 26 uppercase + 26 lowercase + 10 digits + 9 symbols
- Based on "Emission Concentric Circle": center = Sound Source
  - Arching shapes = peace (alpha-waves)
  - Radiating shapes = stimuli (gamma-waves)
  - Vertical/parallel lines = tension (beta-waves)
- Case distinguished by presence/absence of a black dot
- Numbers 0-4 = square waves; pair of dots prefix adds 5
- Free TrueType fonts available from Hymmnoserver

## Hymmnos Script Characters

The 6 main vowel sounds carry fundamental emotional categories:
- A = Power (力音)
- I = Holy (圣音)
- U = Hatred (憎音)
- E = Love (爱音)
- O = Evil (邪音)

The remaining 20 consonants derive from Carmena Foreluna letter meanings.

## Ar Ciela (アル・シエラ)

The planet Ar Ciel's own language, predating all Hymmnos. Each sound carries a feeling; words are sums of letter feelings. Full frequency range: 20-600,000 Hz (30x human hearing).

- Two notation layers: Public (26 symbols, lossy) and Compartment (adds diacritics for inaudible properties)
- FMCL (frequency compartments): `!` `#` `$` `%` for sounds OVER 20,000 Hz (sub-dividing the 20k-600k Hz inaudible range)
- AMCL (amplitude compartments): `&` `(` `)` plus an unwritten fourth (single) for waveform shapes
- Vowels have umbrella categories: A=[Scalar], I=[Pray], U=[Experience], E=[Neighbour], O=[Karma], N=[Mandala]
- Every letter converges on "love" at the spectrum's far end
- Basis of Carmena Foreluna, which is the basis of Hymmnos
