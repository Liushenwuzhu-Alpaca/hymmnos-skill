# Hymmnos Sentence Translation (Without Skill — Baseline)

## Source Sentence
"Was yea ra chs hymmnos mea"

---

## Word-by-Word Breakdown

### Emotion Sounds (Prefix)
The opening three words form a Hymmnos emotion-sound cluster. In Hymmnos grammar, sentences begin with one or more "emotion sounds" (想音) that convey the speaker's emotional state and modify the verb.

1. **Was** — First-person emotion sound ("I"). Indicates the speaker is referring to themselves. In some analyses, "Was" is read as an emotion sound expressing the speaker's own feeling, roughly "I (feel)."
2. **yea** — Emotion sound meaning "happy / joyful / with gladness." It expresses that the emotion driving the sentence is happiness or joy.
3. **ra** — Emotion sound meaning "want to / wishing to / desire to." It expresses volition or wish.

Read together as an emotion cluster: **"I, with happiness, want to..."** (第一人称，怀着喜悦之情，想要……)

### Main Sentence Body
4. **chs** — Verb meaning "to become / to turn into" (成为). This is the main verb of the sentence, governed by the preceding emotion sounds.
5. **hymmnos** — Noun meaning "song / hymn / poem" (歌、诗). Root of the language's own name. As the object/complement here it means "a song" or "hymn."
6. **mea** — Possessive pronoun meaning "my / mine" (我的). It modifies the preceding noun, so "hymmnos mea" = "my song."

---

## Full Translation

### English
"With joy, I want to become a song of mine." — or more naturally: "I happily wish to become my song."

(Literal parse: [I, with happiness, wish to] [become] [song] [my].)

### Chinese
怀着喜悦，我想成为我的歌。
（字面解析：[我，怀着喜悦，想要] [成为] [歌] [我的]）

## Parsing Notes
- The first three tokens (Was yea ra) are parsed as Hymmnos "emotion sounds" (想音), a grammatical prefix that conveys speaker person, emotional valence, and volition respectively. This is a characteristic feature of Hymmnos (the "tower language" from the Ar tonelico game series).
- "chs" is the main verb; Hymmnos typically places the verb immediately after the emotion-sound prefix.
- "hymmnos mea" follows a noun-possessor order; "mea" is a possessive determiner meaning "my," so the phrase reads "my song."
- Overall meaning: the speaker expresses a heartfelt wish — spoken in the first person, colored by joy — to transform into their own song.

### Confidence
- **High confidence:** "hymmnos" = song/hymn; "mea" = my/mine; "chs" = become; "Was" = first-person emotion sound; "yea" = joy/happiness; "ra" = want/wish.
- **Moderate confidence:** Exact grammatical categorization of each emotion sound and whether "Was" strictly encodes "I" vs. a more abstract first-person volition marker. Translations may vary slightly across Ar tonelico source materials.
