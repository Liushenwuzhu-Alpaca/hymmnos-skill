================================================================================
HYMMNOS TRANSLATION — "Was yea ra chs hymmnos mea"
================================================================================

SENTENCE: Was yea ra chs hymmnos mea

SENTENCE TYPE: Emotion Sound sentence (想音句) — Standard Hymmnos (Central
Standard Note), First-Person Self-type. This is the most common sentence type
(~80% of all Hymmnos text), used in EXEC_ songs.

STRUCTURE: [Emotion Sound] + [verb] + [object] + [compound]
            [Was yea ra]  + [chs]  + [hymmnos] + [mea]

The subject "I" is implicit and omitted in self-type sentences (Hymmnos nouns
have no tense or plural marker; the speaker is always "I" when an Emotion Sound
is present).

================================================================================
WORD-BY-WORD BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

--- EMOTION SOUND PREFIX (想音) ---
The three-word prefix encodes the SPEAKER's emotional state. It is always
first-person ("I") and is required for Tower processing (Song Magic).

1. Was
   - Role: First word — Intensity (强度)
   - Pronunciation: rhymes with "sauce"
   - Meaning: "very intensely"
   - Notes: Second-highest intensity level, below Rrha (trance-like, consumed
     with). Indicates the speaker feels the emotion with strong force.

2. yea
   - Role: Second word — Emotion Type (感情类型)
   - Pronunciation: like "yeah"
   - Meaning: "happy"
   - Dialect: Central
   - Notes: The core emotion of the sentence — happiness/delight.

3. ra
   - Role: Third word — Desirability (愿望/持续性)
   - Pronunciation: like "raw"
   - Meaning: "I want this to continue" (default)
   - Notes: The most common desirability word. Due to overuse, "ra" is
     near-meaningless and usually safe to ignore in translation; it conveys
     "let this feeling/state continue."

   → COMBINED EMOTION SOUND: "Was yea ra" =
     "I feel very intensely happy, and I want this feeling to continue."
     (Concisely: "I am very happy" / "delighted")

--- VERB ---

4. chs
   - Role: Verb
   - Pronunciation: —
   - Meaning: "become" / "turn into" / "transform into"
   - Word class: verb (v.)
   - Dialect: Central
   - Notes: The main verb of the sentence. In self-type structure the verb
     follows the Emotion Sound directly.

--- OBJECT ---

5. hymmnos
   - Role: Noun (direct object)
   - Pronunciation: —
   - Meaning: "song" (more specifically, a Hymmnos — a song of emotion/Song
     Magic; also the name of the language itself)
   - Word class: noun (n.)
   - Notes: The thing the speaker is becoming. "hymmnos" carries the nuance of
     a song that expresses emotion or invokes Tower magic, beyond a mere "sarla"
     (ordinary song).

--- COMPOUND ---

6. mea
   - Role: Pronoun (first person)
   - Pronunciation: —
   - Meaning: "I" / "me"
   - Word class: pronoun (pro.)
   - Notes: The object form of the first-person pronoun (mea serves as both
     object "me" and subject "I" — Hymmnos has no true distinct subject form for
     the first person). Here it appears in the compound slot after the object,
     functioning as a possessive/identity marker via juxtaposition:
     "hymmnos mea" = "my song" / "the song that is me."

================================================================================
PARSING SUMMARY
================================================================================

[Was yea ra]  = Emotion Sound: "Very intensely happy, wanting it to continue"
[chs]         = Verb: "become / turn into"
[hymmnos]     = Noun (object): "song (of emotion / Song Magic)"
[mea]         = Pronoun (compound): "me / my"

Literal gloss:
  [INTENSITY: very] [EMOTION: happy] [DESIRABILITY: continue]
  [become] [song] [me/my]
  = "I, very happily wanting this to continue, become [my] song."

================================================================================
TRANSLATION
================================================================================

ENGLISH:
  I am delighted to become a song (to express myself through song).
  / I happily turn myself into song.

  (More fully, accounting for the emotion encoding:)
  "I feel very intensely happy — and I want this to continue — to become a song
   that is myself." / "I am delighted to express myself through song."

CHINESE:
  我非常快乐地化为一首歌（通过歌声来表达自我）。

  (完整译法，含情感编码:)
  "我怀着强烈且希望延续的喜悦，将自己化为一首属于我的歌。"
  / "我满心欢喜地化作歌声，以此表达自我。"

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================

1. This sentence is the canonical example of a Standard Hymmnos Emotion Sound
   sentence, cited directly in the grammar reference
   (grammar-standard.md, line 67):
     "Was yea ra chs hymmnos mea = I am delighted to express myself through song"

2. The Emotion Sound is often omitted in the surface translation because
   English and Chinese convey emotion through word choice ("delighted" /
   "满心欢喜") rather than a separate grammatical prefix.

3. The "ra" desirability word is near-meaningless due to overuse and is usually
   safe to ignore in translation.

4. No tenses exist in Hymmnos; "become" can read as "am becoming" or "will
   become" depending on context. The example gloss uses the present/ongoing
   sense.

5. "hymmnos mea" uses juxtapositional possession (common in Hymmnos): placing
   the pronoun after the noun marks it as "my" / belonging to me. So "hymmnos
   mea" = "my song" / "the song that is me."
